# The Jafurah gas field, 25 Nov 2026: a blaze at a well site, posted as «no facility here»

**What was posted.**
- Post 1345, 19:26 Baghdad time: «🔴 حريق جديد في السعودية», «مؤكد».
- Its note said «لا منشأة مسجّلة في الخريطة هنا، ولم تُرصد حرارة في هذا المكان ولا حوله خلال شهر».
- It was at 25.474 N, 50.190 E, 17 km south-west of Zakhnuniyah island.
- The owner asked whether it was an attack or a gas flare.

**What the pictures show** (`pictures`, `hotspots` through the probe workflow). All times Baghdad.

| Time | Meteosat-12 official fire power | Fire Temperature picture (0.25 km) |
|---|---|---|
| 18:30 and before | – | nothing |
| 18:40 | 13 MW | nothing yet at full detail |
| 18:50 | 64 MW | a rise of 10-16 |
| 19:00 | 9 MW | a rise of 45-90, a warm streak 3.5-3.8 km long |
| 19:10 | 45 MW | a rise of 17-33 |
| 19:20 | – | a rise of 24-53 |

- The 10.5 micron picture did not turn colder over the heat: no thick smoke rose. At Ras Tanura it
  turned 10-15 levels colder from the blaze's second picture.

**What is there.**
- The open map (`osm-near`):
  - a pipeline at 0.5 km;
  - an electrical substation at 0.7 km;
  - oil or gas wells at 1.1, 1.3, 1.3 and 2.3 km, more up to 4.5 km;
  - an oil pipeline at 4.5 km.
- Rasad's facility map holds none of these, and no facility within 45 km.
- The place lies in the Jafurah unconventional gas field: 170 x 100 km between Ghawar and Qatar, being
  drilled by Aramco.

**Its history.**
- NASA's long-term map (197 dates since March 2024) saw heat on this very spot on 6 dates. Cells
  within 2.5 km were hot on up to 10 dates, along an east-west band of about 12 km.
- Rasad's archive holds two events 21 km north-north-west, neither posted:
  - 10 Nov: 65 MW, 8 hours;
  - 13-14 Nov: 45 MW, about 35 hours.

**Reading.** All of this looks like gas burned at a well (a test or a release), not like an accident or
an attack:
- a well site;
- heat that came and went at the same spot over two years;
- no thick smoke;
- a strength that jumped up and down from picture to picture.

The pictures cannot tell the cause. NOAA's night pass, the day's smoke check and a clear Sentinel-2
picture of the pad would tell more.

**What changed.**
- **Wells and pipelines.** The open map's oil and gas wells and pipelines are now kept in their own
  file (`geo/field.json`, `GEO_VERSION` 4) and told in a post:
  - «🛢 في الخريطة المفتوحة: بئر نفط أو غاز على بعد 1.1 كم (وآبار أخرى حوله)، وخط أنابيب على بعد 0.5 كم»;
  - the note then says «لا منشأة كبيرة في الخريطة هنا» instead of «قد يكون موقعاً غير مرسوم».
- **They decide nothing.** Wells and pipelines are never facilities, so no rule sees them.
- **NASA's record.** A new-heat post says NASA's long-term record of the spot: «سجل ناسا منذ آذار 2024:
  حرارة في هذه النقطة في 6 تواريخ من 197».
